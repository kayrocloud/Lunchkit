# Lunchkit
LunchKit is a web-based food ordering platform developed for MSU-IIT students and staff. It provides a centralized platform where users can browse food offerings from participating campus vendors, place orders, and arrange pickups without requiring delivery services.

The project aims to make campus food ordering more convenient while providing participating food vendors with a platform to manage their stores and orders.

**Project Type:** Group Project consist of 4 students from the Computer Science Department
**Development**: Collaborative Development

## Features
- **Food Browsing** – Browse available food items from participating campus vendors.
- **Online Ordering** – Place food orders through the web application.
- **Pickup-Based Ordering** – Arrange order pickups without requiring delivery services.
- **Store Management** – Manage vendor store information and available food items.
- **User Management** – Handle user accounts and application access.
- **Google Authentication** – Support Google account authentication.
- **Email Functionality** – Support email-related application operations.
